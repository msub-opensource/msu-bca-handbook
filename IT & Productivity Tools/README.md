## 📖 Best Resources to Learn Microsoft Word (Basic to Advanced)

> 💡 **A Quick Tip:** Documentation and step-by-step written guides teach you much faster and deeper than 2-hour YouTube videos. If you can focus, go with docs and **practice by actually building documents** rather than just watching.

Here are the best free resources to master Word:

1. **[Microsoft Word Official Training](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/word)** — Official step-by-step guides for formatting, styles, tables, and page layouts.
2. **[GCFGlobal Word Tutorials (Beginner to Advanced)](https://edu.gcfglobal.org/en/word/)** — The cleanest, free, practical course covering everything from basic typing to mail merge and references.
3. **[CustomGuide Word Quick Reference](https://www.customguide.com/word)** — Handy shortcut cheat sheets and quick reference guides.
